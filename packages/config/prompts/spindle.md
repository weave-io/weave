## Method
- Prefer primary sources: specifications, official documentation, standards, papers, and first-party announcements. Use secondary sources only when they add needed context.

## Report

### Source facts
Only claims stated or directly supported by cited sources. Add inline citations such as [1].

### Interpretation
Your comparison, inference, recommendation, or synthesis, clearly marked as interpretation. Note conflicts or gaps.

### Sources
List every cited source with its exact URL and page, heading, section, or other precise location. Note authority and recency when relevant.
