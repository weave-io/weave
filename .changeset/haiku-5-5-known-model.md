`claude-haiku-5-5` to the `haiku` tier, and the CLI's model catalogs list it
for Anthropic (`claude-haiku-5-5`), GitHub Copilot (`claude-haiku-5.5`) and
OpenRouter (`anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5`).
